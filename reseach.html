Exploring light–matter interactions at the atomic scale.

We combine scanning tunnelling microscopy, optical spectroscopy and nanoscale materials science to investigate and control electronic and optical phenomena at the ultimate length scale.

02

Our approach

Scanning probe microscopy

STM and related scanning probe techniques provide access to electronic structure, molecular excitations and nanoscale interactions with atomic spatial resolution.

Light at the nanoscale

We combine tunnelling spectroscopy with optical excitation and photon detection, including STML, STM-LE, TERS and TEPL.

From molecules to quantum materials

Our systems range from individual molecules and molecular assemblies to graphene nanoribbons and two-dimensional materials, with a particular focus on engineered quantum states and quantum emitters.

```
```
03

Experimental frontier

Our experimental platforms combine cryogenic scanning tunnelling microscopy with advanced optical excitation and detection. Current developments include ultrafast and tunable laser excitation, high-resolution optical spectroscopy and time-resolved measurements at the nanoscale.

The goal is to access light-induced electronic and optical phenomena with unprecedented spatial, spectral and temporal resolution.
